Power meters

Choose the power sensor (in W) of each phase of your whole-home meter. Single-phase homes need one entry. If your meter also reports the power factor (Shelly 3EM, ...), choose it too: it tells heating elements from motors. It is optional, suggested where the names match, and does not restart the analysis. Devices that already have their own meter (smart plugs, ...) can be assigned to their phase: their power is subtracted, so they are not "detected" again.

Devices with their own meter

Important for good results: a device that already has its own meter (heater, kitchen appliance, printer, ...) is otherwise "detected" again. If you don't know the phases, let Shadow Meter find them from the history: it looks at which phase changes whenever a plug's device switches.

Saving restarts the analysis. If you change the meters of the phases, the analysis starts over; devices you confirmed from lights are kept.

History

The detected devices are discovered anew: their names, types and confirmations are lost, and devices you added to Home Assistant from patterns are removed there. Devices confirmed from lights are kept.
